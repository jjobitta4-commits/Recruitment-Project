5 Core Innovations

Engineering-focused algorithmic capabilities designed for high-precision talent acquisition and candidate career acceleration.

Innovation 1

Smart Job Matching

Multi-factor weighted match score: Skills 45%, Experience 20%, Education 10%, Projects 10%, and Assessment 15%. Provides strong matches, missing skills, and recommendations.

Innovation 2

Skill Gap Analyzer

Compares candidate skills against job requirements, identifies missing critical skills, and automatically generates a sequential step-by-step learning path with priority tiers.

Innovation 3

Candidate Ranking

Recruiters view applicants sorted by calculated algorithmic match scores, with multi-criteria filters for skills match percentage, assessment scores, and verified years of experience.

Innovation 4

Adaptive Assessment

Dynamic test engine adjusting question difficulty in real-time: correct answers scale up to Medium & Hard, incorrect steer to Easy, producing holistic proficiency scoring.

Innovation 5

Career Recommendation

Analyzes declared skills, certifications, and assessment history to recommend industry career trajectories (Backend Architect, Full-Stack Lead, DevOps Specialist) and skill readiness levels.
